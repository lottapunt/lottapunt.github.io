Fines under the EU AI Act

Potential fines

The applicable maximum depends on the infringement, the organisation and whether it qualifies as an SME.

Up to €35M or 7%

For the most serious infringements, including prohibited AI practices.

Up to €15M or 3%

For many other violations of AI Act obligations.

Up to €7.5M or 1%

For incorrect or misleading information supplied to authorities.

This is a short summary, not legal advice. Lower maximum amounts may apply to SMEs and start-ups.
