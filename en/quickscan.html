ShowAI AI Act QuickScan

A clear assessment of how your organisation uses AI.

The QuickScan maps selected AI use cases, identifies potentially relevant AI Act requirements and converts the findings into a practical, prioritised action plan.

Request a QuickScan

What the QuickScan examines

  • Purpose of each AI system or tool
  • Departments, employees and external parties involved
  • Data entered, generated or shared
  • Whether AI supports work or influences decisions
  • Human oversight and approval
  • Relevant AI Act themes and possible risk classification
  • Existing policies, training and documentation
  • Missing or disproportionate controls

What you receive

  • Management summary
  • AI-system inventory
  • Assessment by department and use case
  • Relevant AI Act themes in plain language
  • What is already organised well
  • Risks, gaps and unresolved questions
  • Concrete recommendations
  • Prioritised action plan

What the organisation provides

The QuickScan uses proportionate input. It does not require a complete export of every prompt, chat or document.

  • A short intake
  • A list of selected AI tools and use cases
  • Interviews with relevant owners or users
  • Representative examples where needed
  • Existing policies, training materials or process documents

Clear boundaries

The QuickScan is an assessment, not a formal audit, legal opinion, conformity assessment or compliance certificate. Complex legal questions are clearly marked for specialist review.

A positive conclusion is still useful

A QuickScan may show that heavy obligations are not relevant to a selected use case. The report still provides an inventory, current controls, future checkpoints and proportionate recommendations.

Method

A focused assessment, not a formal audit.
