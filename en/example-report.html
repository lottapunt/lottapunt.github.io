Management summary

Northline Studio uses several AI tools across marketing, operations and recruitment. Most use cases are assistive and supported by human review. The recruitment screening use case requires the highest priority because its purpose may trigger high-risk requirements and needs qualified legal confirmation.

Example findings

What is already working

Named tool owners, human approval for external content and restricted access to selected tools.

Priority gap

No central inventory or standard process for assessing new AI tools before adoption.

Uncertainty to resolve

The recruitment tool’s intended purpose, vendor role and human influence require deeper assessment.

Proportionate action

Start with an inventory, tool-approval process, role-specific AI literacy and targeted documentation.

Prioritised action plan

0–30 days

Assign an AI owner and confirm the recruitment use case.

30–60 days

Adopt an approved-tools list and basic internal AI policy.

60–90 days

Deliver role-specific AI literacy and formalise review checkpoints.
